# Frame 0.8.1

- **Check for updates** now sits in the right-click menu, just above Settings. Before, it was hidden inside More → About Frame.

Coming from 0.8.0? Right-click the picture and choose **Check for updates**, or run the new setup over your install. Your settings stay.

Coming from 1.0.0 or 1.0.1? The update checker in those versions will not offer 0.8.1, so download it by hand this once and run the setup over your install.
